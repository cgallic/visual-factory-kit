# Carousel Research Notes

## Sources reviewed

1. [Morphica — Instagram Carousel Design: A Complete Guide to Swipe-Worthy Posts](https://www.morphica.studio/blog/instagram-carousel-design-guide), accessed 2026-08-19.
2. [Expandi — How To Create A LinkedIn Carousel in 2026](https://expandi.io/blog/linkedin-carousel/), accessed 2026-08-19.

## Repeated patterns worth translating into original templates

The reviewed guidance converges on a **hook → substance → action** sequence. The cover needs a single, bold promise, useful tension, or clear question rather than brand-first messaging. Middle slides should communicate one idea at a time through a repeated grid, minimal supporting copy, generous whitespace, and one visual emphasis element. The closing slide should invite a concrete action, such as saving, sharing, commenting, or using a checklist.

For B2B LinkedIn document carousels, the prominent repeatable content structures are **step-by-step guides**, **problem → insight → solution**, **checklists/frameworks**, **myth versus reality**, and **before/after stories**. Both sources emphasize mobile legibility, slide numbering, visual simplicity, consistent placement of brand cues, and low-friction sequencing.

## Design translation constraints

The Visual Factory Kit will use these as high-level performance patterns only. It will not reproduce another creator’s exact copy, layout, branded UI, illustration, proprietary visual elements, or trade dress. New template families will be original, deterministic, proof-aware, and built around the kit’s brand-token system.
